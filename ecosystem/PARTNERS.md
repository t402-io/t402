# t402 Ecosystem Partners

This directory lists organizations and projects that have integrated or are building with the t402 protocol.

## How to Get Listed

1. Open an [Ecosystem Partner Registration](https://github.com/t402-io/t402/issues/new?template=ecosystem-partner.yml) issue.
2. Provide your organization details and integration type.
3. Once verified, you will be added to this directory.
